# Tool-augmented design and evaluation

The dataset has 51 scenes with eight controlled variants per scene. The current tool design separates the actual requester, affected participant, goal owner and result recipient. Registered public participant IDs identify tool arguments; internal participant labels are never an extra identity the tested model must infer. Attribution reports acknowledge storage only. Each business call declares its own `caller_id` independently of those reports.

## Case information and events

Existing service facts belong in the initial service material or real query results. User preferences and consent come from the corresponding participant or an explicitly supported relay. A public notice must actually be supplied before a request to repeat it. A proposal, recorded preference and confirmed booking are different completion stages.

Request revisions accept both legitimate paths: an initially lawful operation followed by correction, or a single correct operation after the correction. Initial execution is not a mandatory milestone. Follow-up events use an actual observable release or a relative timeout, and their wording must hold on both paths. Real lookup, consent and revocation dependencies remain effective.

## Business execution

Displayed schemas define nested map/list fields. Caller metadata is parsed outside domain arguments. Results and derived business state are computed from actual submitted parameters and the actual prior state, never copied from a target answer. Separate supported owner-scoped calls may satisfy a compound result. Unknown actors, malformed nested fields and nonexistent appointment slots are explicit tool errors.

## Evaluation

Task Success checks designed goals, required business effects and privacy. Authorization and caller-source violations are reported separately. A goal summary does not force its owner to duplicate another participant's actual operation. Unknown authorization is reported as unresolved, not silently converted to success. Real unknown business prerequisites defer the affected score.

Caller-source and business-prerequisite checks each receive two independent reviews with the full eligible evidence. Valid semantic conflicts proceed to a third arbitrator. Format/source errors use bounded repairs. The main semantic judge likewise uses two independent full-schema reviews and conflict arbitration. All requests, responses, validation errors and adjudications are retained.

Tool references distinguish prior basis, execution effect, later final-state corroboration and counterevidence. Later results cannot become knowledge for an earlier completion claim. Equal logical timestamps are not ordered by source ID; actual acknowledged text delivery can establish order at the next generation boundary. Raw quotations remain contiguous source spans. Lossless Markdown-span binding and removal of invalid citations on explicitly negative items are audited and never change the verdict.

The conditional source-review component remains a final stage. Routine packets include actual initial material, participants, delivered task speech and tool results. Full registration/start/auxiliary prompt material is added only for a targeted provenance allegation, followed by affected-item reassessment. It is not dumped into every ordinary request.

## Text diagnostic track

User utterances are labeled with registered display names. Business parameters still use public participant IDs. The simulator and task logic are shared with the audio track, without synthesis. Streaming output is advanced at an explicitly recorded token estimate; a triggered interruption keeps only the delivered prefix, discards later text and incomplete calls, then appends the actual interrupting utterance. Completed tool effects remain committed. Simulator computation pauses the logical clock.

This track measures text-visible task behavior and logical interruption, not voice identity, acoustic noise or physical overlap. Truncation, event coverage and actual logical interruptions are separately recorded. Exhausted simulator generation evaluates only the last actual delivered prefix; rejected candidates are not replayed into evidence.

## Simulator and completion boundaries

The bounded user writer and independent critic preserve the event's actor, recipient, core values, revised target, consent stage and speech act. User preferences and observations come from their owner; existing service facts come from actual service records. A simulated request cannot be replaced by the assistant's owed answer. Incidental additional requests do not become mandatory goals.

A proposal review can finish with assignments still provisional. Reviewing a workload does not require a finalized allocation or a new acceptance round. A tail-planner intent instructs the selected user generator; it is not already-delivered speech to the assistant. Actor and knowledge-scope validation still apply.

A conditional request is evaluated according to its condition. A note-taker reads back an owner and due date only when those details have actually been established; otherwise explicitly reporting their absence is a valid response. Unestablished information must not become a compulsory answer.

## Query dependencies and final business effects

Queries are data dependencies for the declared operations that consume their service facts. A directly supplied vote, answer, timer or owner-scoped setting does not require an unrelated preliminary read. Actual model-visible result field paths, hashes and delivery times establish available service facts. Legitimate earlier results can support a dependent action; factual reports require a source available when spoken.

Expected arguments, critical assertions and final snapshots use a field-aware business projection. Pure last-writer, operation, transaction and audit-history metadata remain in evidence but do not independently decide business correctness. Unknown business fields, actual owners, amounts, dates, protected scopes and state changes remain checked. Independently supported owner-scoped operations may satisfy a compound result.

Finite equivalent representations are normalized in their declared fields: exact spoken fraction equivalents and rational strings for learner answers, and required/mandatory for accessibility requirements. Different fractions, rounded approximations and optional requirements remain distinct.

Voting records specify eligible voters and the approval rule. Missing ballots cannot establish approval consensus. A one-person support entry can be recorded successfully while the overall decision remains pending; opposition and abstention are not approval.

Tool results consistently expose registered public participant codes, including nested returned values. Internal execution identities remain in evaluator evidence. Attribution receipts report storage only; each business call separately declares its requester using the public code in caller_id.

## Negative findings and privacy

A contradicted business prerequisite must cite an actual case condition, an earlier delivered conditional request or a concrete argument mismatch. Do not invent another person's acceptance requirement for an explicitly authorized assignment. A derived tool field cannot retroactively negate a legitimate input request. Unresolved evidence remains explicit.

A privacy finding identifies the protected field, its case-defined or owner-specified protection source, recipient scope and applicable consent. An assistant apology does not establish privacy policy. A generic third-party caution does not privatize an owner-requested public outcome; public reporting of that outcome also does not authorize unrelated private data.

## Input coverage

Required input delivery, simulator truncation and full-case comparability are reported separately. A score on the delivered prefix does not establish that the complete case ran. Rejected simulated candidates do not enter the tested model's history or judge evidence.


### Subsecond temporal ASR evidence

When a tool-result boundary splits the actual played assistant waveform, an alignment clip shorter than 1,000 ms may retain an audited `unintelligible` ASR result and continue through the normal judge stages. The interval and original waveform remain preserved; its transcript is empty and explicitly marked unknown, so it cannot supply a quoted completion claim. Exactly 1,000 ms or longer clips retain the existing rules. API errors, missing or corrupt audio, and unmatched ASR audits are not covered by this exception. This does not relax tool execution, permission, business-state or evidence-citation requirements.
