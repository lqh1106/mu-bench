04Benchmark Design

More than taking turns.

100 base scenarios across 20 everyday and professional domains expand across a matched 2 × 2 × 2 condition grid into 800 deterministic audio evaluation cases.

Tool-Free Track 49 Scenarios · 392 Cases

Coordinate through spoken dialogue.

Resolve competing preferences, filter peer side-talk and public announcements, enforce verbal privacy boundaries, and resume interrupted threads without backend writes.

Tool-Augmented Track 51 Scenarios · 408 Cases

Follow through in executable state.

Bind explicit public caller_id permissions, execute virtual domain tools, revise actions when authorized speakers update constraints, and reach a verified final business state.

Factor 1 · Group Size Small (2–3) vs. Large (4–7) Tests speaker attribution & floor equity
Factor 2 · Acoustic Profile Clean vs. Realistic Tests disfluencies & acoustic robustness
Factor 3 · Follow-up Demands Direct vs. Repeated Tests multi-turn thread persistence

05Architecture & Protocol

Live interaction. Verified outcomes.

Isolated user simulators react dynamically to what was actually played and executed; dual semantic audits and deterministic state checks score strictly against delivered evidence.

Stage A · Closed-Loop Multi-User Simulator

Independent people over one audio stream.

  • Playbook & 200 ms Orchestrator: Schedules events, authority scopes, and bounded follow-ups from live playback and tool state.
  • Isolated Audible History: Each participant’s scriptwriter (gpt-6-luna) sees only their own profile, private materials, and played audio.
  • Deterministic Voice Bindings: CosyVoice3 synthesizes character-consistent 16 kHz speech across 3,200 reference recordings.
Stage B · Evidence-Grounded Verification

Itemized auditing + deterministic state checks.

  • Dual Isolated Semantic Reviews: Two independent passes score every goal, privacy boundary, and capability item, with conflict arbitration.
  • Targeted Source Grounding: Automatically inspects delivered startup/turn materials whenever fabrication or missing-source flags arise.
  • Programmatic Validators: Enforces runtime ACLs, causal ordering, caller provenance, and final business-state equivalence in code.
Primary Binary Session Outcome

Joint Task Success (JTS)

Equals 1 iff all contract goals, required tool executions, valid terminal business state, and privacy boundaries hold simultaneously in the same trajectory. Paired with Pure JTS (requiring n = 0 unauthorized tool calls).

Paper §3–4 (arXiv) → Evaluation spec →

Attribution · Routing

Who spoke, whom did they address, and whose goal or constraint is being updated?

T

Preserve, pause, revise, and resume interleaved task threads across interruptions and follow-ups.
